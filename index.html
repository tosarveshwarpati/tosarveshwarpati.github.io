About me

Physics Scholar with good skills in Quantum Theory, Mathematical Methods and Optical Experiment design at graduate level. Interested in both Theoretical and Experimental Physics.

I work in laser cooling and spectroscopy laboratory at Warsaw University for production and precision spectroscopy of ultracold CsAg molecule. I absolutely love my work.

What I'm doing

  • Density Matrix based Spectroscopic Calculations: Optical Bloch Equations offers a precise way to calculate spectroscopic properties of finite(few) dimensional quantum systems.
  • Programming: Junior Physicists do a Lot of Programming. (but senior physicists/supervisors dont!)
  • Electronics And Control: A PhD student's life is filled with a myriad of electronics and control system design work
  • Spectroscopy / Imaging: Ultimately I am performing laser based spectroscopy and imaging experiments, life is over.

Fun Stuff

Apart from these research activities, I have also volunteered to popularize/teach some physics courses to the engineering (but also science) students who want to come to higher studies in physics. The courses I have taught during the last two years are Lagrangian Physics, Second Quantization, and Fourier Transforms. I am also a teaching assistant to a currently running online course on quantum field theory, organized by Infosys Science Foundation.

My secondary interests also involve foundations and representations of QM, basis-dependent units of complex scalar fields elements, Hilbert spaces, Linear Algebra, old quantum theory, design/demonstration of home experiments, extraction of motivational/philosophical concepts from mathematics (Parseval-Plancherel identities, CSCO, Completeness, etc.), Linux bash, electronic circuits...
